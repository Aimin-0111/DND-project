## The Old Mill

- **Id** : old_mill
- **Region** : Mill Road, beside the Copper River
- **Mood** : abandoned, damp wood, stale flour and a whiff of recent smoke
- **Sounds** : the river, the stopped wheel creaking, crows
- **Danger** : low by day, rust wolves at night
- **Items to find** : rusty_sword, healing_draught
- **NPC here** : oda

Oda's mill has not turned since last winter. Yet for three nights the villagers
have seen an orange glow behind the shutters.

The side door stands ajar. Inside, flour covers everything and a faint smell of
smoke still hangs in the cold air.
